# G520 in the research lab: a recall deferred for want of a stall opens no visit (0802), 2026-11-06

## What it is (traced 2026-11-06 between 08:47 and 08:52 UTC)

A car out at work is called home in three places: the twin's deployed-telemetry step (AP-4,
`twin.ottoq_sim_advance_deployed_telemetry`), `ottoq_decide_return_on_signal`, and `ottoq_plan_dispatch_tick`'s
`surplus_to_demand` recall of an overnight hold. Each books the appointment first through `ottoq.ottoq_book_appointment`,
which opens the car's visit (`ottoq_sim_generate_service_manifest` → `ottoq_derive_visit_needs`) before it looks for a stall.
When the need can wait and no stall is free the booking answers `secured: false`, and every caller leaves the car out at
work to try again next tick. The visit stays. The same tick, the visit step's last statement
(`twin.ottoq_sim_advance_visit_atoms`) closes every visit whose car is `deployed`, so it closes `complete` with nothing done.
The deployed-telemetry step stamps the car's `last_state_change` every tick, so the next attempt's visit has its own clock
and key, and another opens. No stall is booked by these visits, so they take no capacity. The scorecard counts each one as
a visit still in the depot at the horizon.

## The fix (db/migrations/0802, PENDING)

The work in `ottoq_book_appointment` from opening the visit to the stall search runs in a block of its own, rolled back
whole when the search finds no stall for a need that can wait and this call opened the visit: the visit, its plan and
itinerary, the car's manifest and charge plan. The car stays out at work as before. Its visit opens on the attempt that
secures a stall, or at once for a need that cannot wait (unchanged). A visit the car already had open is never touched.
Applied to a copy of the lab template (`ottoq_lab_0802` = `ottoq_lab_0801` + 0802), every check in the file passes, V2
included: a twin car booked against a depot with no stalls opens no visit when the need can wait (0 → 0) and opens one
when it cannot (0 → 1). The patched body's md5 is ed54d6d4.

## What 0802 is not

It changes no decision about a car: when a car is called home, which stall it is given, and the deferral itself (a need
that can wait and finds no stall still keeps the car out at work, as `ottoq_evaluate_return_need`'s ladder escalates it)
are all as before. Past runs keep their phantom rows; a scorecard read of one still counts them (the lab's readers leave
them out). In production, with real cars, a deferred return used to leave its visit open from the first attempt (no twin
visit step closes it); with 0802 the visit opens when the car is actually called home.
