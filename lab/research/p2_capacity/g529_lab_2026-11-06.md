# G529 in the research lab (2026-11-06; LAB ONLY)

**The question.** The first demo run since 0803 (640e4fab, 2026-11-06 19:14-20:59 UTC, 5.26 sim-hours) logged the battery
forecast's evidence on every tick: 1,249 rows. That run is one fold, a run's local day. The refit (`ottoq_bess_forecast_refit`,
cron 802 at 5:26 AM CT) writes a usable model at 3 folds and 200 examples at each half hour ahead, h = 0..7. The plan was
two more demo runs of about one sim-hour each. Would those make an honest model?

**No.** A model's upper band at h is the 80th percentile of the out-of-sample misses at h: each fold's examples graded by a
fit on the other folds. A fold has examples at h only where its run went on 30h + 30 minutes past the example's clock (6
snapshots in that half hour). The bar counts folds over all half hours, never per half hour. So a half hour that one fold
reached has no left-out fit, no misses, and a band of `COALESCE(NULL, 0)` = 0 kW. The point forecast is stored as the
upper band (cv.n 0), and the model still passes.
