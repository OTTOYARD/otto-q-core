# G526 in the research lab: a demand-response call holds cars from chargers on the gate's guess, with the battery 90% full (2026-11-06)

## Found

In the battery planner study (`planner_0801_lab_2026-11-06.md`) two of ten 0801 base days had cars that a battery plan
could move. Seed 717171's base day run a second time is the same day to the record (`b801r`), so the twin reproduces; the
two days part at 2:40 PM CT, in the middle of a demand-response call that began at 2:25 PM (the only one-tick difference
in the decide tick's frame was the grid import, 566.5 against 571.5 kW, which the battery moves). During a call the
decide tick's 0132 gate admits a charge only while the charging cars' committed kW plus the request stays under
`ottoq_ev_charge_allowance_kw`: the call's cap less the building plus solar plus what the battery can sustain to the
call's end (0433). The battery's state is in that sum, so a different battery admits different cars; and on the base days
the gate deferred cars (`deferred_site_power_cap`) though the battery stood 88-91% full.

**The cars are held on a guess.** The gate counts each charging car at `LEAST(connector kW, inlet kW or 250)` times a band
of its charge (0.85 under 55%, 0.55 under 75%, else 0.30); 0727 (2026-10-17) measured that guess at a median 1.50 times the
metered power (p90 1.96) and noted that "at ten chargers the gate never binds". At thirty posts during a call it does.
0727 built the dial `site_cap_gate_metered` (count a charging car at its charger's last metered power) and left it off; it
has never been switched on at the twin depot (production read 2026-11-06 09:25:35 UTC: no depot-scope row; only rows on
old sweep runs).

**The call.** The twin's igniter (`twin.ottoq_sim_maybe_ignite_dr_call`) seeds its roll, duration and reduction on the
depot and the run's clock, not the run's seed, so every run draws the same call at the same moment and only the
afternoon's temperature decides whether it fires: on 717171 (32.4 C) and 282828 (33.0 C) it came at 2:25 PM, asking a
183.3 kW reduction from the prior hour's mean grid draw (caps 534 and 230 kW) for 192 minutes; the other eight seeds
stayed under 32 C and had none.

## What it says

A demand-response call is the utility's, and the depot honours it: the battery carries what the cap leaves (0433). It
does not need to hold a car to do it. On every one of these calls the battery could have carried the reduction many times
over (183 kW for 3.2 hours is about 590 kWh; the battery stood near 2,400 kWh above its floor), and with the gate counting
real power it did, holding no car and keeping the call. **The fix exists: switch 0727's dial on at the twin depot**
(`site_cap_gate_metered` = 1), a finished change that rule 12 puts on at the twin and rule 9 asks for (a car is never held
on a guess). It changes nothing on a day the gate does not bind.

What it does not settle: whether a call that the battery cannot carry should ever hold a car (rule 13 says the cars' needs
come first; a demand-response program is a contract the depot chose). None of these eight calls came close to that.
