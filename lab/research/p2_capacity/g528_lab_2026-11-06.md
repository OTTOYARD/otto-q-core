# G528 and G527 in the research lab (2026-11-06; LAB ONLY)

**The question.** 0804 (the battery planner's seam) and 0805 (its daily grade) were measured on lab days that tick every 5
minutes, the planner's own step. Read against production after both were applied (2026-11-06 17:50-17:53 UTC), 55 of the 57
operator demo runs started at the twin depot in the last 60 days ticked under 5 sim-minutes (median 14.7 sim-seconds a tick)
and production's one session ticked every 103 seconds (`ottoq_sim_runs`, sim span over tick count, read 18:03:36 UTC). Two
defects follow, both latent (every dial off, the edge function undeployed):

- **G528.** The planner's fire started a plan one step after its clock, and the orchestrator follows the newest ready plan
  at step floor((clock - start) / step). At a tick shorter than the step the next tick reads step -1, and since every tick
  fires a newer plan, no plan is ever followed. **0806** starts the plan at the fire's own clock, asks the planner at most
  once per step of sim time, and averages the tick under way with the snapshots already in the last past step.
- **G527.** The grade rounded the mean tick to whole minutes for its step and its 30-minute window. Under 30 seconds that is
  0 minutes; at production's latest run (edf611ad, 74 snapshots over 14.6 sim-minutes) it billed a 15-minute slice at a
  whole day's demand rate. **0807** reads the day in 5-minute steps of sim time (time-weighted), whatever the tick.

## 0807 (G527)

- **At the 5-minute tick it grades exactly as 0805**, on a copy of `424242_b36` (run e3907922) with 0805 then 0807 applied:
  the window from 6 PM day 1 (realized $1,539.61, 1,019.4 kW at 11:00 PM, as `bound_energy.py`; no battery $1,497.50) and
  both local days, 0 of 903, 687 and 686 values differing.
- **Its V2** (one load at a 15-second tick and the next day the same load averaged over 5-minute ticks, 8 to 11 AM) grades
  both days alike: realized $651.12 and peak 786.5 kW in both, no battery $647.12 in both; a part-day of 1 hour 50 minutes is
  not graded.
- **At the 2-minute tick** (`b36t2`, the 6 PM window): energy $765.55 and wear $6.61 as `bound_energy.py` and as 0805; the
  peaks 998.4 kW (realized) and 1,166.0 kW (no battery) against 1,000.4 and 1,169.2, because 30-minute windows on a 5-minute
  grid cannot start at every 2-minute offset; realized $1,484.35 against $1,485.76. 0805 itself reads a 2-minute tick
  correctly (step 2, window 15 snapshots); it breaks under 30 seconds (step 0) and on a tick that is not whole minutes (103 s
  rounds to 2, a 26-minute window).

## Limits

One seed at the 2-minute tick: a single reading, not a range. Production's own demo runs tick much faster than 2 minutes;
at 15 seconds a lab day of 36 hours would be 8,640 ticks, and 0806's V2 checks the step arithmetic at 15 seconds directly.
