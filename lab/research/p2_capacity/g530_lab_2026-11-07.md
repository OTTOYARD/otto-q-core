# G530 in the research lab (2026-11-07; LAB ONLY)

**The question.** Model 8 has been the battery's forecast since the planner went on at the twin depot (11:43:39 UTC).
Its own record says that out of sample it misses more than the day plan's own EV forecast at 6 of the 8 half hours it
covers. Why, and what should the refit do instead?

**The answer.** The refit's ridge is fitted at a fixed lam = 10, which suited six 36-hour lab days and overfits short demo
runs. The demo runs are 3 to 5 1/2 sim-hours, each from 8 AM. So a half hour ahead draws its examples from one narrow window
of each run's day, ticks 15 seconds apart nearly copy each other, and a fold is a run's day: thousands of rows but only five
independent days. Choosing the regularization at each half hour by the band's own out-of-sample loss fixes it here and
changes nothing on the 36-hour days. 0809 builds that, and also defers to the day plan's forecast at any half hour where
that missed less.

## The evidence, and that the lab holds it exactly

- Production's `ottoq_bess_forecast_log` for the five runs model 8 read (640e4fab, 9af1b226, 3091816d, 5c19fd60, 73d71553):
  4,599 rows, pulled read-only at 11:52 UTC into `ottoq_lab_0809` (a copy of `ottoq_lab`). Fingerprint md5 `015c14d5` on
  both sides.
- Production's refit (prosrc `a142840c`) on those rows in the lab rebuilt model 8 exactly: 5 folds, 23,881 examples, every
  weight, mean, spread and band identical (largest difference 0), the same cv and the same profile.
- A Python replica of the ridge and its leave-one-fold-out split (`cv.py` in the session scratchpad) matched model 8's
  misses and bands to the 0.1 kW. Every candidate below was scored with it.

## What does not fix it

The day plan's own EV forecast as a tenth feature: h = 1 goes from 443.8 to 429.0 kW, and h = 2..7 move by under 4 kW.
The fit is the defect, not its inputs.

## What grades a band, and the numbers

The battery plans on the 80% upper band (forecast plus the 80th percentile of its out-of-sample misses), so the score is the
band's own loss: the 0.8 pinball loss of each miss less the band (kW, lower is better).

| h | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | sum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| lam 10 (model 8) | 71.7 | 132.3 | 127.9 | 103.1 | 125.6 | 94.8 | 79.3 | 123.9 | 858.6 |
| day plan, with its own band | 240.7 | 95.9 | 102.1 | 100.8 | 93.4 | 80.2 | 61.6 | 50.4 | 825.1 |
| best lam per half hour (10 to 10^9) | 62.0 | 80.2 | 86.7 | 77.9 | 69.4 | 47.9 | 37.1 | 35.9 | 497.1 |
| chosen on the other folds alone (nested) | 62.4 | 79.8 | 87.8 | 77.9 | 92.2 | 47.9 | 65.4 | 37.9 | 551.2 |

- The loss falls as the shrinkage grows, all the way to lam = 10^9 at h = 1..3 and 6..7. On this evidence no feature beats
  the depot's average EV draw at that horizon out of sample, beyond the next half hour. At h = 0 the features do carry
  signal (best at lam 10^4).
- The nested figure is the honest one for the procedure: 551.2 kW, 35.8% under lam 10 and 33.2% under the day plan. It
  trails the day plan at h = 6 (65.4 against 61.6) and ties it at h = 4.
- The bands narrow from model 8's 217 to 823 kW to 119 to 352 kW.
- The point miss (MAE) at the chosen regularization still trails the day plan's at h = 1..3 (360, 386, 356 against 345,
  324, 340 kW). What the battery reads is the band, and there the model is better at every half hour.

## The six 36-hour lab days (`ottoq_lab_0803m`, 2,592 rows, 6 runs)

lam 10 is the best at every half hour (summed loss 375.9 kW against the day plan's 939.4), and the nested split picks it 96
times in 96. With 0809 applied to a template copy holding those rows (`ottoq_lab_0809r`), the refit picks lam 10 at every
half hour and writes the same model: 0 of 8 half hours differing apart from the new keys, the same profile, by run's day (12
folds) and by run (6).

## A different day defeats every forecast here

On the normal_day run (5c19fd60) the depot's mean EV draw 1 to 3 hours ahead was 0 to 173 kW (h = 1: 173, h = 2: 23,
h = 4 and 5: 0). Every forecast ran 1,100 to 1,700 kW above it on average: the day plan by +1,229 to +1,716, model 8 by
+1,123 to +1,363, and the heavily shrunk fit by +1,144 to +1,290. Four busy days and one normal day cannot teach a forecast
what a normal day looks like. That run also graded 7.3% (planner off). It is the next finding, not this one.
