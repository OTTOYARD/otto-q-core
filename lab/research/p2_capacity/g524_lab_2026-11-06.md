# The battery day plan's own forecast, graded in the research lab (G524), 2026-11-06

The battery's day plan (`public.ottoq_bess_day_plan`, 0435/0444/0601/0797) plans against a forecast of the depot's load,
half hour by half hour: the building's profile, the sun, and the EV charging, which it forecasts as the largest of three
terms (0444):

- **known** (`ottoq_forecast_ev_known_kw`): two loops. (1) The cars on a charger now, each held at its starting rate until
  its energy is in. (2) Every car coming home, charged at 60% of its inlet from its arrival, **whether or not a post is
  free**.
- **queue** (`ottoq_forecast_ev_queue_kw`): the cars waiting on site and coming home, queued onto the posts as they free,
  each at the rate its battery accepts (0601).
- **persist**: the last hour's draw, held flat.

## How it reads against what the depot drew

`capacity_day.py --log-forecast` reads the day plan's forecast after every tick (a STABLE read that changes nothing: the
audited normal day served 253 visits, as its base) and `forecast_grade.py` sets each term against the EV charging the
depot then drew, half hour by half hour. Two whole 12-hour days at 116 cars, the lab template as it stands (production's
engine through 0797, its twin dials at production's), read 2026-11-06 between 07:23 and 07:24 UTC:

| next half hour, forecast less drawn: bias / mean absolute (kW) | normal day, seed 171717 | 2 PM call day, seed 171717 |
|---|---:|---:|
| **as the day plan reads it**: max(known, queue, last hour) | **+1,228 / 1,228** | **+1,210 / 1,210** |
| known (cars on a charger now + cars coming home) | +1,228 / 1,228 | +1,210 / 1,210 |
| the cars on a charger now alone | +193 / 233 | +187 / 229 |
| the post-aware queue | +219 / 224 | +280 / 285 |
| the last hour's draw | +27 / 240 | +28 / 253 |
| the rule without the post-blind arrivals: max(on a charger, queue, last hour) | +314 / 317 | +354 / 357 |

Further ahead the day plan's rule is off by +109 to +261 kW on average (mean absolute 270-480 kW): there the last hour's
draw sets the maximum, and the post-blind loop no longer dominates. The first half hour is the one the plan acts on: it
re-plans every tick, and two ticks into the normal day its forecast peak was 4,706 kW (2,135 kW of it the cars on a
charger, the rest the cars coming home, onto 28 of 30 posts already busy) against 2,431 kW drawn, and it set its level
at 3,248 kW.

**The defect**: the second loop charges every car coming home from its arrival with no regard to the posts, and the
maximum takes it exactly when the depot is congested. The post-aware queue term (0444, G177) already queues the cars
coming home onto the posts, so the second loop adds nothing the queue does not, except the error.

## The fix, priced (pending: four lab days queued 07:25 UTC)

`whatif_dayplan_on_charger.sql`: the day plan reads the cars on a charger now (loop 1, verbatim, as
`ottoq_forecast_ev_on_charger_kw`) in place of the known term; queue and the last hour as before.
