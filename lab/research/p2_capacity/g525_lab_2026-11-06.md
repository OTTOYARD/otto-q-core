# 0801 (G525's fix) in the research lab, 2026-11-06

**The defect.** `twin.ottoq_arm_registration_check` draws the car's true pose at a robotic post (lateral, vertical, yaw)
and the marker detection from the arm cycle's id, which `twin.arm_cycles` mints with `gen_random_uuid()` on every run.
So a seed does not reproduce its plug-ins. On two unpatched runs of normal_day at 116 cars, seed 424242
(`..._fcbase`, `..._g517on`), none of the 54 plug-ins that line up by car, post, time and attempt drew the same pose
(the first: lateral -13.66 mm against +31.75 mm), read 2026-11-06 07:58 UTC. Mostly it changes nothing a car feels; when a
pose falls outside the arm's reach the plug restages, and on a fourth run (`..._lp3learn`) one did (1 of 257): the car
left its post a tick early, the seating changed from 11:55, and the day ended with 5 fewer charging sessions and 11 fewer
visits served (217 against 228).

**The fix.** `db/migrations/0801_the_arms_registration_draw_is_keyed_on_what_a_run_reproduces_not_on_the_cycles_minted_id.sql`
(file md5 1c06bcf0, PENDING): the key becomes the car, the post, the cycle's direction and its start in sim time
(`twin.ottoq_sim_clock_salt`), one pose per cycle and a fresh detection per attempt as designed. Applied to a copy of the
lab template (`ottoq_lab_0801`, patched body md5 3e2bfb19 = the file's V3); its V2 probe (two cycles of one plug under two
minted ids draw one pose) passes on the patched function and fails on the unpatched one.

**The gate.** The same seed twice on `ottoq_lab_0801` (`jobs_2026-11-06_g525.txt`, written by 08:01:35 UTC):

| normal_day 116 / 424242 | run a | run b |
|---|---:|---:|
| charging sessions (start, car, post, status, end, energy) | 262 | 262, **identical** |
| arm registrations (car, post, time, attempt, true pose, verdict) | 270 | 270, **identical** |
| restages | 0 | 0 |
| visits served | 228 | 228 |

Rule 9: no car's need is touched; a plug's chance of restaging is unchanged in distribution, and which plug restages is
now the seed's. Lineage forces_recert and forces_dial_restart TRUE (the twin's draws change); rule 12 holds both.

**And through retries (read 2026-11-06 08:12 UTC).** The detection draw is keyed on the cycle id too, a fresh look per
attempt. Three unpatched runs of normal_day 116 / 717171 restaged nothing but retried 9, 11 and 10 plug-ins, and their
charging sessions part at 19:40 (session 181): a retry costs the plug time, and the seating moves from there. Until 0801 is
applied, any lab comparison of two runs of one seed can differ by this alone.
