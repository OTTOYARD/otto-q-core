`track_functions` is `pl` for **every session of role `postgres`** (`ALTER ROLE postgres SET track_functions = 'pl'`,
set at about 12:05 UTC for this measurement and reset after it). pg_cron runs every job as `postgres`, so the delta
counts the tick path (the metronome, cron 12, and the depot tick), and also every other cron job that fires in the
window. The recertification runner and the dial runner do no work while a run is live, so no pair contributes.
Calls through PostgREST (the edge functions, the cockpits' RPCs) log in as `authenticator` and are **not** counted:
the role setting applies to the login role only.
