## FINDINGS

The Waymo depot family is now fully mapped and current: the parent granted in February, one
continuation is docketed for examination, the parking-prediction sibling took a first office action,
and the EP counterpart number is confirmed. The important strategic read is unchanged and now
*stronger with evidence*: every claim in the granted depot patent is **vehicle-side** (a prioritized
staging-area list computed onboard, observing bays before committing) — its own specification
position rejects centralized stall assignment. No new filing this cycle claims **multi-tenant,
cross-operator arbitration or settlement** (target area a). The closest new filings — a Siemens
bus-fleet dispatch/charging patent and a Cummins depot peak-power patent — are both single-fleet,
single-operator; they read on energy-constrained joint service scheduling (target area b) only in
the narrow sense, and neither touches cross-fleet coordination. Recall-timing optimization (target
area c) remains unclaimed as a first-class object. The three provisional target areas survive this
cycle's scan unowned.

### 3. Target-area same-day alert (excerpts + overlap reads)

> **Alert (a) — multi-tenant arbitration + settlement: NO new filing.**
> No application published this cycle claims allocation of a shared service point among two or more
> unaffiliated operators with a signed settlement record. Overlap read: the target remains open; the
> nearest prior art (US 2021/0086647 A1, multi-customer charging control) is five years old and
> does not claim settlement. This is the cleanest L2 opening on the board.

> **Alert (b) — energy-constrained joint service scheduling: TWO adjacent single-fleet filings.**
> **Siemens US 2026/0236859 A1** (pub 2026-08-13): *"assigning a charging duration factor (CDF) to
> each EV … based on a respective charging requirement … and an available charging infrastructure of
> the EV fleet depot"* and *"optimizing the charging schedule to maximize a number of EVs … having
> fully charged and balanced battery storage."* Overlap read: this is the OTTO-Q "when does each
> asset charge, against a shared feed, so it is ready" question — but solved for a **single** bus
> fleet with a route-dispatch objective, not a joint multi-operator service bundle. It does not
> claim arbitration, settlement, or cross-fleet coordination.
> **Cummins US 12,583,354 B2** (granted 2026-03-24): *"perform a peak power optimization analysis to
> generate a vehicle charging profile … vehicle-to-vehicle power transfer."* Overlap read: shared-feed
> peak management at a depot with V2V transfer — again single-operator, no service-event model.

> **Alert (c) — recall-timing optimization: NO new first-class claim.**
> Waymo's depot continuation (US 2026/0145703 A1) is the closest object and it is vehicle-side
> staging, not a recall-decision optimizer. No assignee claims "decide when an asset leaves its work
> cycle for a service bundle" as the primary object.

### 4. Freedom-to-operate concern list (ranked by severity)

Research triage for counsel — not a legal opinion. Rankings reflect *claim proximity to OTTO-Q's
three target areas*, not an assessment of validity or infringement.

1. **Waymo depot family (US 12,545,288 B2 + US 2026/0145703 A1 continuation)** — *moderate, monitor.*
   Granted + actively prosecuted. But claims are vehicle-side staging-list behavior, and the granted
   spec's background affirmatively disclaims centralized stall assignment — the opposite of OTTO-Q's
   central multi-tenant kernel. Risk is to a *vehicle-side* implementation, not to the kernel.
2. **Siemens US 2026/0236859 A1** — *low, watch.* Single-fleet dispatch+charging (CDF). Overlaps the
   energy-constrained scheduling *shape* but no cross-fleet/arbitration claim. Filed 2026 — watch for
   broadening into multi-operator territory.
3. **Cummins US 12,583,354 B2 / US 12,233,739 B2** — *low, watch.* Depot peak-power + V2V, single
   operator. No service-event or multi-party language.
4. **Waymo US 2026/0179491 A1 (parking prediction)** — *low.* Street parking; only a concern if claims
   drift toward depot lot occupancy prediction. Non-Final Action mailed 2026-05-07 — claim set is in
   flux; re-check next cycle.
5. **Rocsys portfolio (robotic charging → cleaning/inspection expansion)** — *low, but rising.*
   Watch for bay/service-point scheduling claims as they extend past charging.

**Net:** none of the three provisional targets (multi-tenant arbitration + settlement;
energy-constrained *joint/multi-operator* service scheduling; recall-timing optimization) is claimed
by any filing surfaced this cycle.
