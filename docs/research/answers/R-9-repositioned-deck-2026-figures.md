## Q2 — The physical bottleneck: sites, power, interconnection, 2026

_Labeling key: **(a)** primary/official · **(b)** standards/academic · **(c)** trade press/analyst · **(d)** inference. Every figure is 2026-dated unless flagged with an explicit `vintage` tag. `NOT FOUND` = no defensible number located._

### 1. Large-load interconnection timeline, 2026

- **FERC large-load orders — primary/official, June 18 2026 (a).** FERC issued six show-cause orders (FPA §206) to all six RTOs/ISOs (PJM, MISO, SPP, CAISO, NYISO, ISO-NE), directing them to justify or reform interconnection, network-upgrade cost, and transmission-service terms for large loads (data centers and other ≥50 MW users). FERC calls it "aggressive targeted action" to speed large-load integration. — https://www.ferc.gov/news-events/news/ferc-launches-aggressive-targeted-action-speed-large-load-integration · https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/ferc-orders-grid-operators-promptly-revise-or-justify-interconnection-rules-data

- **Time-to-energize a 200+ MW load: "at least 5 years" — academic, vintage Dec 2025, carried (b).** LBNL "Queued Up 2025 Edition" (Dec 15 2025): ≥5 years from interconnection request to commercial operation for large loads (200+ MW). No 2026 refresh of this specific load figure found; the FERC June 2026 orders are the regulatory response aimed squarely at this backlog. — https://emp.lbl.gov/queues (2025 PDF cited via American Action Forum, June 2026: https://www.americanactionforum.org/insight/ferc-data-center-orders-accelerate-grid-connection/)

- **"5–10 years" (GridLab/Elevate, 2025) — analyst, vintage 2025, flagged carry (c).** Carried without a 2026 refresh; no 2026 GridLab/Elevate update located (`NOT FOUND`). Closest 2026 context = the FERC June 2026 orders + LBNL 2025 "≥5 years" above.

- **GENERATOR-QUEUE figure — academic, 2026 edition (b). MUST NOT be read as load timeline.** LBNL "Queued Up 2026 Edition": ~8,200 projects seeking transmission interconnection at end-2025 = 1,312 GW generation + ~749 GW storage; explicitly **excludes load interconnection requests**. Label: `generator-queue, not load`. — https://emp.lbl.gov/publications/queued-2026-edition-characteristics · https://emp.lbl.gov/queues

### 2. Data-center load competition, 2026

- **EPRI 2026 — standards/academic (b).** "Updated Scenarios of U.S. Data Center Electricity Use": data centers projected to consume **9%–17% of U.S. electricity by 2030**, up from 4–5% today. — https://www.epri.com/research/products/000000003002034696 · https://powering-intelligence.epri.com/

- **EPRI 2026 state-level capacity — standards/academic (b).** 2026 data-center capacity (Low/Med/High): **42.2 / 58.5 / 73.8 GW** nominal; 2026 peak load **29.7 / 35.5 / 41.1 GW**. — https://powering-intelligence.epri.com/dashboard/

- **LBNL — academic, vintage 2025, carried (b).** Data centers could reach **11.8% of U.S. electricity by 2030** (2025 update). LBNL's next update was expected during 2026 per E&E News — not yet located (`NOT FOUND` as of this write). — https://eta.lbl.gov/publications/united-states-data-center-energy-2025

- **Grid Strategies — analyst, vintage Dec 2025 (c).** Five-year national peak-load growth forecast **166 GW by 2030**; data centers ≈55% of that growth. Grid Strategies' own alternative benchmark: ~65 GW data-center growth through 2030 (flags utility forecasts as overstated by ~25 GW). — https://gridstrategiesllc.com/project/load-growth-forecast/

- **ERCOT large-load queue — primary/official, Q2 2026 (a).** ERCOT's large-load interconnection queue (non-generator, tracked separately from the generation queue) reached **466 GW in June 2026**, nearly doubling from March; **data centers = 90.2%** of tracked load (ERCOT's own June 18 2026 classification). **Supersedes the deck's Q1 2026 figure.** — https://www.ercotqueue.com/charts/large-load · source: ERCOT Large Load Interconnection Status Update, June 19 2026
