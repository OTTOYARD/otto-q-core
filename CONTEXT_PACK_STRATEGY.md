## 1. The thesis (memorize this)
OTTOYARD's defense play is a **neutral sustainment/regeneration orchestration layer that sits UNDER mission C2**. Every hull-builder ships its own command software (Saronic Echelon, Anduril Lattice); nobody neutral orchestrates the **node** — the pier, expeditionary littoral site, or mothership deck where heterogeneous USVs/UAS/ground assets return to recharge, refuel, rearm, offload data, and get health-scored and re-slotted. The government is deliberately buying heterogeneous fleets and will not hand one prime its rivals' telemetry — multi-OEM neutrality is therefore compliance-aligned (MOSA is statutory; UMAA is the Navy's USV interface standard), not just positioning.
- **North-star metric:** sortie regeneration rate (sorties per asset per 24h through the node). Supporting: turnaround time (recovery→ready), asset uptime, peak kW avoided.
- **Concept name:** OTTO-X Littoral — containerized sustainment node variant of OTTO-X (FARP/air, ground AV, energy island, data offload, maintenance, cross-dock modes).
- **Business model:** capital-light. Partners supply hulls, launch/recovery hardware, energy hardware; OTTOYARD supplies orchestration software + reference design.
- **We orchestrate, never actuate.** Telemetry in → prediction/scheduling → "when, where, why" directives out. No motion control, ever.
