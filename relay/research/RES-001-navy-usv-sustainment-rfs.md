## 3. Requirement language (verbatim from SAM.gov description)
- "Request for Solutions: The Navy desires to establish an enduring, self-sufficient operational and sustainment model for its rapidly evolving fleet of Unmanned Surface Vessels (USVs)."
- **Purpose:** "The Navy is acquiring multiple new USV platforms with short lifecycles (approx. 5 years), but Fleet units lacks an agile tool to establish and manage the organic training, maintenance scheduling procedures, and sustainment readiness knowledge to operate these systems independently. The current hand-tailored, vendor-dependent support model is not scalable…"
- **Objective (deliverables):** prototype and operationalize a **vendor-agnostic sustainment and training framework** for multiple USV platforms — standardized **O-level maintenance procedures**, execution-procedure capture, **corrective maintenance support requests**, **data separation between/among platforms**, a **fleet-wide training curriculum + Joint Qualification Requirement (JQR)**, and **agile lifecycle-management processes**; end state = Fleet personnel conduct maintenance/operations with **minimal-to-no vendor support**.
- Final line of the notice: **"Please go to matac.org for additional details."** *(a) primary — this is the link that makes it a MATAC OTA RFS, not a FAR solicitation.*
