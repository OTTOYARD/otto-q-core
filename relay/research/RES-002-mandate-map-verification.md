**§6 Q3 — Does MAP spawn fundable autonomy-infrastructure programs?**
- YES it spawns named vehicles, NO standing money for a pre-seed autonomy-infra company today.
- Maritime Security Trust Fund: (a) EO 14269 — "the OMB Director shall, in coordination with the Secretary of Transportation, develop a legislative proposal… to establish a Maritime Security Trust Fund… a reliable funding source… for MAP programs." It is a LEGISLATIVE PROPOSAL (needs Congress), not an enacted fund. Press characterizes it as a "sovereign wealth fund" funded by new cargo fees / tariff revenue ((c) Thompson Hine; (c) HK Law). Not appropriable yet.
- Maritime Prosperity Zones (MPZs): ~100 tax-advantaged zones (coasts, Great Lakes, rivers, AK, HI, territories) modeled on 2017 Opportunity Zones — tax incentives to attract private capital into shipyards/supply chain/workforce ((c) Blank Rome, MAP p.6). This is a tax vehicle for investors, not a grant a company files for; a pre-seed autonomy firm benefits only indirectly.
- Shipbuilding Financial Incentives Program: (a) EO 14269 — Transportation legislative proposal for grants + FCRA loans/loan guarantees, but scoped to shipyards/repair facilities/drydocks and vessel construction — not autonomy infrastructure.
- Autonomy angle: MAP says "Fostering the development of the autonomous maritime technology industry" and "invest in R&D for modular and robotic ship [building]" and "autonomous maritime technologies" — policy goals, not funded solicitations. Verdict: the autonomy pillar is regulatory, not funded; the funded vehicles (MSTF/MPZ/SFIP) target shipbuilding/industrial base/ports. A pre-seed autonomy-infrastructure company has NO directly pursuable money today; closest adjacent paths are (1) SFIP shipyard/repair grants+loans, (2) MPZ private-capital incentives, (3) watch the Treasury/Commerce MSTF governance plan (one tracker: 90-day plan). (d) inference on adjacency.
