# RES-003 — Army xTech + AFWERX Open Topic windows
FOR: CHASE
ANSWERS: R2, R3

TL;DR:
- R2: The one OPEN Army xTech door right now is **xTech|Search 10** (flagship open-topic; white papers Aug 5–Sep 30, 2026) — up to $1M cash + up to $4.8M SBIR/STTR, and its priority portfolios explicitly include **Contested Logistics & Sustainment** and AI/ML. **xTechPrime is NOT currently open** (not listed on the primary site).
- R3: The traditional AFWERX "propose-anything" Open Topic has been **retired for 2026** and replaced by "Focused Open Topics"; the live window is **26.BZ (SBIR) / 26.TZ (STTR) Release 5** (pre-release Aug 5, open Aug 26, close Sep 23, 2026). Phase I max is now **up to $323,000** (not the legacy $75K).

FINDINGS — R2 (Army xTech):

**OPEN (as of Aug 26, 2026):**
- **xTech|Search 10** — flagship open-topic prize competition. White paper/Phase 1 window **Aug 5 – Sep 30, 2026**. Prize: **up to $1M cash prizes + up to $4.8M in Army SBIR/STTR proposals**. Eligibility listed as "Small and large U.S.-based and allied foreign country businesses" (broader than typical SBIR). Four priority portfolios: Sensors; Immersive & Wearables; AI/ML; **Contested Logistics and Sustainment**. Details (finalist counts, Phase 2–4 dates, RFI PDF) were still placeholders ("Coming Soon", "Winner Announced: Jan 1, 1970") at access time — treat as not-yet-finalized. [a1][a2][a3]

**Follow-on SBIR windows (finalists/winners ONLY — not open to the general public):**
- **xTech|Phantum** (quantum sensors/photonics; cash-prize phase CLOSED, winners Jul 31): SBIR proposal window **Aug 10–28, 2026** via DSIP; Phase I SBIR up to **$300,000**, ≤6 mo PoP. Up to $200K cash prizes (16 finalists @ $2.5K, up to 8 winners @ +$20K). [a4][a6]
- **xTech|Inversion** (Army IP commercialization; CLOSED Aug 20): SBIR proposal window **Sep 8–25, 2026**; Phase I SBIR up to **$300,000**. Up to $1M cash prizes (12 finalists @ $20K; finals winners @ $152K). Eligibility: for-profit, ≤500 employees, US-citizen/permanent-resident owned. [a5]

**ACTIVE (submission closed, competition in progress):** G-TEAD USARPAC ACE 27-01 (Autonomous Sustainment), xTech|Kinetic Reach ("agile sustainment", closed Jun 30), xTech|Apex Intercept, xTech|Adaptive Strike, xTechHumanoid. [a2]

**xTechPrime:** NOT FOUND on the primary xtech.army.mil competitions list as of access date. Only a third-party aggregator (grantedai.com) references "xTechPrime (2026)" with "up to $15,000 for selected teams" — undated/unverified. Treat as no currently-open primary window; recheck xtech.army.mil/competitions/. [a2][c1]

**Eligibility & prize structure (general):** xTech competitions use cash-prize authority (10 U.S.C. § 3201); winners/finalists may then be the *only* firms eligible to submit a follow-on SBIR/STTR proposal. Cash prize → then SBIR contract (not an outright grant/contract at entry). [a1][a4][a5]

FINDINGS — R3 (AFWERX Open Topic SBIR):

**Big change:** the traditional "propose-anything" Open Topic is **gone for 2026** — AFWERX replaced it with **"Focused Open Topics"** (Air Force names the capability gap, solution approach stays open), stated on an April 2026 AFWERX webinar and expected in every 2026 release. SBIR authority lapsed Sep 30, 2025 and was reauthorized spring 2026 (through FY2031). [c2]

**Current/next windows (primary):**
- **DoW SBIR Specific Topic 26.BZ Release 5** — pre-release **Aug 5, 2026**, open **Aug 26, 2026**, close **Sep 23, 2026**. [a7]
- **DoW STTR Specific Topic 26.TZ Release 5** — same dates. [a7]
- Topics pre-release on the **first Wednesday of every month** (rolling). [a7]

**Phase I award size:** **up to $323,000**, up to 6 months PoP (current, afwerx.com). Phase II up to **$2.1M** / 24 mo. NOTE: legacy Open Topic figures were $75K (SBIR) / $110K (STTR) Phase I, $2M Phase II, $1.25M D2P2 — those appear on a trade page flagged "Inactive" and should NOT be quoted as current. [a7][c3]

**Submission checklist (what a company needs ready):**
1. SAM.gov registration (required before award). [a7][a8]
2. Submit via **DSIP** (dodsbirsttr.mil/submissions) — cover sheet + Technical Volume + Cost Volume + Supporting Documents (Vol 5) + Fraud/Waste/Abuse training. [a8][a9]
3. Supporting docs: STTR Research Institution Teaming Agreement (REQUIRED, STTR); key-personnel resumes; signed DoD Funding Agreement Certification; Allocation of Rights; DD Form 2345 (ITAR/EAR, if applicable). [a9]
4. **Customer Memorandum** (identify AF/SF customer + end user) — the Open/Focused-Topic gate to Phase II. [c4]
5. Eligibility: for-profit US small business, **≤500 employees**, >50% US-owned; PI >50% employment with the SBC; SBIR Phase I ≥2/3 of effort by the proposing firm. [a7][a8]
6. FY26 AFWERX themes: Autonomous Mass; Command/Control & Battle Mgmt; Counter Incursion; **Agile Combat & Readiness (incl. Contested Logistics)**. [c2][c3]
