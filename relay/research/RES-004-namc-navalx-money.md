# RES-004 — NAMC membership, NavalX Tech Bridges, adjacent money
FOR: CHASE
ANSWERS: R4, R5, R10

TL;DR:
- NAMC is the pipe: $500/yr flat dues (prorated Oct 1–Sep 30), online app w/ DD2345+UEI+CAGE, COO+Exec-Committee review ≈5–7 business days; current OTA is the DAI OTA (Army GVSC/ACC-Detroit Arsenal), no cost share for small-business/NDC primes.
- There is NO NavalX "Midsouth" Tech Bridge — the closest relevant bridge for a Nashville sustainment/logistics company is the Midwest Tech Bridge (Crane, IN), not Gulf Coast (Panama City, FL, maritime/coastal). No current named per-bridge director found.
- OSC (loans/loan-guarantees) and APFIT ($10M–$50M procurement) are both explicitly LATE-STAGE — not usable by a pre-seed; nearest pre-seed money is TN LaunchTN SBIR/STTR match (up to 50%) and NC DEF-TECH/Commerce matching grants.

FINDINGS:

## R4 — NAMC (National Advanced Mobility Consortium)
- Structure: independent 501(c)(3) non-profit, member-led; ~550 US member orgs (prime→small biz, NDCs, academia). No foreign members (US subsidiaries allowed). (a)
- FEE: flat $500/year, prorated for new members on gov fiscal year Oct 1–Sep 30. No other fees; member events free. Pay by Visa/MC/Amex/check/ACH; auto-renews Oct 1. (a)
- PROCESS (online application): FEIN, UEI, CAGE, DD2345 (required upload), FOCI mitigation plan (if relevant), 4 contacts (key/contractual/financial/technical), special designations (WOSB/VOSB/etc.), attest to ≥1 technical capability area, past-performance awards, non-proprietary capability narrative PDF (no CUI), optional logo. (a)
- ONBOARDING TIME: reviewed by NAMC COO, approved by Board Executive Committee; "typically 5–7 business days unless more information is needed." (a)
- OTA TERMS: GVS OTA expired Sept 2023; current = DAI OTA (Detroit Arsenal Innovation), awarded Sept 2023, administered by Army Ground Vehicle Systems Center (GVSC) + ACC–Detroit Arsenal. 15 technology focus areas; vehicles = RPPs, RFIs, Task Requests, Annual Plan topics; many offer follow-on production. OTA is non-FAR/DFARS. (a)
- COST SHARE: $0 if Prime is an NDC, a non-profit, or the whole team is small business. Traditional prime w/o significant NDC/SB participation pays 1/3 cost share. IP negotiated per-project. (a)
- Scale: 400+ projects competed exclusively via NAMC; ~$250M/yr expected project value; 73% of funding to NDCs; 1-in-4 members awarded. (a) — consistent with CONTEXT_PACK that Army Project Sustainment runs through NAMC.

## R5 — NavalX Tech Bridges (nearest for Nashville)
- FALSE PREMISE: there is no "Midsouth" Tech Bridge in the NavalX network. "Mid-South" in a Navy context = NSA Mid-South (Millington TN, near Memphis), a Navy HR center, NOT a Tech Bridge. (a/d)
- Network: NavalX launched Tech Bridges Sept 2019; 15 bridges as of Mar 2021 (navsea "Tech Bridges 101"); grown to 18 by 2024–25 (DVIDS). Anchored mostly by NAVSEA Warfare Centers. (a)
- Closest to Nashville: Midwest Tech Bridge (Crane, Indiana — M&S, power/energy, comms, UAS, microelectronics, AI, cyber, hypersonics; anchored NSWC Crane) vs Gulf Coast Tech Bridge (Panama City, FL — coastal science, assured maritime access, met/oc; anchored NSWC Panama City). Midwest is ~half the distance and topically matches SUSTAINMENT/LOGISTICS; Gulf Coast is maritime/coastal and a poor thematic fit. (a/d)
- DIRECTOR / POC: network-level = Whitney Tallarico (co-founder/director, 2021) and NavalX lead Capt. Casey Plew (2024). Current named director for the Midwest or Gulf Coast bridge specifically: NOT FOUND in primary pages searched (navsea.navy.mil, navy.mil, dvidshub.net, secnav.navy.mil). (a)
- INTRO PATH: no public intake form found; accepted route is (1) the bridge directory/annual report at secnav.navy.mil/agility/Pages/techbridges.aspx, then (2) contact the anchor Warfare Center (NSWC Crane for Midwest; NSWC PCD for Gulf Coast) or NavalX central; vehicles are tech transfer, SBIR/STTR, and consortia (incl. NAMC). (a/d)

## R10 — Adjacent money
(a) OSC — Office of Strategic Capital (OUSD R&E, est. Dec 2022). Funds critical-tech SUPPLY CHAIN via debt + equity, not R&D grants. Two instruments: (i) Credit Program direct loans up to $150M (US manufacturing facilities); (ii) SBICCT Initiative — fund-level leverage up to $175M/fund (SBA SBIC + DoD "Green Light"). Eligibility = US-domiciled company supporting one of 31 Covered Technology Categories (FY24 NDAA §903). EXPLICITLY INELIGIBLE: early-stage / pre-revenue / insufficient operating history w/o a creditworthy sponsor. Pre-seed engagement = raise from an SBICCT-licensed fund (equity) or wait for scale; first Equipment-Finance NOFA closed Feb 3, 2025. (a)
(b) APFIT (Accelerate Procurement & Fielding of Innovative Technologies) — OUSD(R&E)/ASD(MC) program, FY2022+. Awards $10M–$50M PROCUREMENT funds to projects that COMPLETED development and are ready to field. NOT self-nominated: requires a government sponsor (Service/COCOM/Agency) nominating via their APFIT organization lead. Status: FY26-2 closed Dec 3, 2025 (selections ~late spring 2026); FY27-1 opens ~early summer 2026. FY26-1 had 76 viable proposals/$2.36B; FY25 selection rate 30% (18/60). Cumulative >$1.4B to >75 cos. Contact osd.apfit@mail.mil. Pre-seed: NOT applicable now (needs completed dev + sponsor). (a)
(c) State programs —
  TN: LaunchTN SBIR/STTR Matching Fund (matches up to 50% of federal SBIR/STTR award; e.g., $100K Phase I → up to $50K) + free application support; TNECD FastTrack Economic Development Fund (job-creation, not defense-specific). No TN-specific defense grant found. (a)
  NC: NC DEF-TECH (Defense Technology Transition Office, deftech.nc.gov; part of NCMBC; POC TJ Gilroy, gilroyt@ncmbc.us) — matching grants (NC: 67 grants >$4.6M in FY2022); NC Commerce Technology Development Grant (SBIR/STTR matching + incentive; FY24-25 pool $444,444); NC Defense Manufacturing Community Support Program (DMCSP) $7.47M (defense advanced textiles, NC State). (a/c)
